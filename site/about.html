Our story
1996Tong Hin, Singapore

Since 1996, Tong Hin has been a trusted name in insurance. That legacy continues today as TH Insurance Brokers, where clients always come first.

We focus on unbiased advice, competitive coverage and quality service that protects what matters most. We don't just sell insurance. We build enduring relationships founded on trust, integrity and reliability, backed by consistent results and continuous innovation.

Our vision

To be the trusted partner that guides clients through risk, empowering individuals and businesses to stay protected, resilient and confident in every challenge.

Our mission

To protect and give peace of mind to our clients by delivering tailored insurance solutions and proactive risk guidance, so they can focus on growing their businesses and building secure futures.
