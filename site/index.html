Renewals, handled

The policy that lapses is the one nobody reminded you about.

Most people think about insurance twice: when they buy it and when they need it. We close that gap. THIB tracks every expiry you hold with us and starts the renewal before you would have remembered.

01

We message you first

A WhatsApp reminder sixty days before expiry, from the team that handles your policy.

02

Your details are already filled in

Last year's details are carried over. You change only what is different and send it back.

03

We compare and come back with options

Quotes from our panel, side by side, with the premium and excess set out plainly.

04

Pay by PayNow or card, and the policy arrives

Your policy lands in the portal and in your chat the moment payment clears.
