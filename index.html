Renewal platform · prototype · October 2026

The renewal platform, ready to click through.

A working prototype of the renewal and retention platform for TH Insurance Brokers. Clients get reminded before a policy lapses, renew in a couple of minutes with their details already filled in, compare quotes, pay, and have the policy delivered. Staff get one desk for all of it. Three parts, below. Everything is clickable; the names, policies and amounts are invented.

Try the whole journey in five minutes

Open the client portal and the staff admin in two tabs of the same browser. What a client does in one appears in the other.

  1. In the portal, choose Create your account. Try Continue with Singpass: your details and vehicle arrive from Myinfo, so there is nothing to type.
  2. On the road tax step, see the amount worked out from the log card, with the basis shown, and choose who renews it.
  3. Finish. In the admin, the new client appears under New sign-ups with the reminder date already scheduled.
  4. Open Verify log card, then try Email the contact on a business sign-up: pick a template and send, and watch it land in the Inbox.
  5. Browse Clients for the full book, personal and business, fifty a page, and Users & access to invite a colleague.
About this prototype. Nothing is sent, stored on a server, or charged: messages, payments, Singpass and email are simulated in the browser. Quotes attach illustrative premiums to real insurer names. Reviews are THIB's own Google reviews, shown as written. The specification this prototype follows is held separately.
